🛡️ Privacy at a Glance
Smart Budget does NOT collect, track, sell, or transmit your financial information to any external server. All transactions, credit card numbers (last 4 digits only), bank names, debts, envelopes, and income history are stored strictly in an encrypted/local SQLite database on your physical device.

1. Core Offline Architecture

Smart Budget ("the App", "we", "us", or "our"), developed by Dravid Sajin, operates under an Offline-First Privacy Model.

Unlike traditional cloud fintech applications that require bank sync APIs or store your transactions on remote databases, Smart Budget is designed to function entirely standalone. The database resides inside your mobile device’s sandboxed private storage (smart_budget.db via SQLite). We do not operate remote telemetry servers, tracking databases, or user profile stores.

2. Information You Provide & How It Is Handled

All information entered into the application is provided voluntarily by you to facilitate personal budgeting, expense calculation, and debt payoff projection:

Data Type Examples Where It Is Stored
Transactions & Ledger Amount, date, category (e.g. Food, Fuel), payment mode, custom notes. Device Local SQLite only
Credit Cards & Bills Bank name, card network, last 4 digits, billing date, due date, limit. (No CVV, PIN, or full 16-digit PAN is ever collected). Device Local SQLite only
Debts & Loans Principal, interest rate, tenure, EMI, payoff milestones. Device Local SQLite only
Digital Envelopes & Goals Monthly category allocations, rollover amounts, savings targets. Device Local SQLite only
Paycheck Allocations Income frequency, waterfall fixed bills, guilt-free daily safe spend. Device Local SQLite only
App Preferences Base currency symbol, theme (dark/light), notification schedules. Device Local AsyncStorage

3. Device Permissions & System Access

The application requests minimal permissions strictly necessary for native Android features:

  • Biometric Authentication (USE_BIOMETRIC / USE_FINGERPRINT): Used solely to unlock the app using your device's fingerprint or face scanner. Authentication is executed entirely by the Android OS Hardware Security Module (BiometricPrompt). The app never receives, stores, or accesses your actual biometric data.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used exclusively to deliver locally scheduled reminders on your device (e.g., daily expense logging reminder, weekly budget review, or statement cycle wrap-up). No remote push notification servers or device tokens are used.
  • File Storage & Export: Used when you explicitly choose to generate and export an Executive Monthly PDF Statement or a local JSON database backup file to your device's download folder.

4. Third-Party Sharing, Advertising & Tracking

We uphold a zero-monetization policy on personal user data:

  • No Ad Networks: Smart Budget does not contain any advertising SDKs (e.g. Google AdMob, Unity Ads, Meta Audience Network).
  • No Data Brokers: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your financial records to data brokers, financial institutions, or marketers.
  • No Third-Party Analytics: We do not use third-party user behavioral trackers (e.g. Mixpanel, Amplitude, Adjust).

5. Optional Cloud Backup (Google Drive)

Smart Budget includes an optional backup feature to allow you to safeguard your data across app reinstalls:

  • User-Controlled: Cloud backup is completely optional. If disabled, the app operates 100% offline.
  • Client-Side Encryption: Before uploading, your backup file is encrypted on your device using industry-standard AES-256 with your custom passphrase.
  • Private Storage (appDataFolder): Backups are saved directly into your personal Google Drive hidden app-data scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata). The file is completely private to your Google account and is inaccessible to other apps, third parties, or the developer.

6. User Control & Permanent Data Deletion Rights

In strict compliance with Google Play User Data Policies, you have absolute control over your personal data:

  • 1-Tap Instant Erasure (In-App): You can permanently wipe all records at any time. Navigate to Settings ➔ Security & Data ➔ Reset All Data. This immediately drops all tables and permanently destroys all records stored in the SQLite database.
  • Uninstall Deletion: Because all data is stored locally in the application's private sandboxed directory, uninstalling Smart Budget from your Android device immediately purges all application data from device storage.
  • Cloud Backup Deletion: If you created an encrypted Google Drive backup, you can delete it directly through the app or by removing Smart Budget from your Google Account's Third-party apps with account access dashboard.

7. Data Security Architecture

We implement multiple layers of security to safeguard your local financial information:

  • Sandboxed OS Storage: The SQLite database is created in Android's private app storage (/data/data/com.dravidsajin.smartbudget/), which cannot be read or accessed by other installed applications.
  • AES-256 Encryption: Database backups exported or synchronized to Google Drive are encrypted using AES-256 encryption.
  • Biometric Lock: App launch can be restricted with biometric or device PIN authentication to prevent unauthorized physical access.

8. Children's Privacy

Smart Budget is a personal financial management tool intended for individuals managing their personal or household budgets. The app does not target, collect, or solicit personal information from children under 13 years of age.
